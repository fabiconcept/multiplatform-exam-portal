###### **01 — OVERVIEW** 

#### Project **Overview** 

The proposed application will be a CBT and exam preparation platform designed to help students practice, take examinations, and track their performance. The first version will focus on Android, Windows, and Web, with iOS planned for a later phase. The goal is to launch with the core features needed for a strong CBT experience while leaving room for the platform to grow. 

###### **02 — DELIVERY** 

#### Platforms 

The first version will support: 

|**01**|**02**|**03**|
|---|---|---|
|ANDROID|WINDOWS|WEB|



The Windows application will support offline use, making it suitable for students who may have limited or unreliable internet access. iOS can be introduced in a later phase.

###### **04 — ACCESS** 

#### Web **Platform** 

Students will be able to access the CBT platform directly through a web browser. 

###### **05 — OFFLINE USE** 

#### Windows Offline **Support** 

The Windows application will support offline examinations. Prepared examination content can be made available to students so they can take examinations without requiring a constant internet connection. Results and other relevant information can be synchronized when the application reconnects to the internet. The exact offline workflow will be finalized during the planning stage.
